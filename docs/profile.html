Job titles

A job is shown only if its title contains one of the wanted terms. Short terms catch more (“strategy” matches “Head of Strategy” and “Strategy Manager”). The grey number shows how many jobs on the current shortlist contain the term.

Wanted titles

At least one must appear in the job title.

Ranked lower −25 points

Still shown, but further down – your areas of lesser interest.

Never show

Jobs with these words in the title are dropped.

LinkedIn search terms

Short list used for the “Check on LinkedIn” links.

Seniority

Levels you are aiming for. Jobs at these levels get full points, neighbouring levels get partial points.

Countries & towns

A job is shown if its location contains one of these names. Add both local and English spellings (München, Munich). Jobs without a stated location are always kept.

Places

Countries, regions, cities or country codes.

LinkedIn search location

One place for the LinkedIn links (e.g. Finland, Helsinki, Germany).

Skills & competences

Professional and personal skills are scored separately, each with its own weight (see section 6). The number shows how many jobs on the current shortlist mention the skill.

Professional

Fields, technologies, methods, markets, languages. Default: up to 30 points, full points at 6 matches.

Personal

Leadership, ways of working, strengths. Default: up to 10 points, full points at 3 matches.

Bonus keywords up to +5

Topics you especially want (e.g. P&L, AI, international).

Your CV text is stored as the GitHub secret JOBFINDER_CV_TEXT and adds up to 10 points for overall similarity. Secrets can't be displayed – to update it, paste the new text under Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions in the repository. To turn CV text into skills, use section 5.

Generate from text

Paste any text – a CV section, a reference letter, notes on what you want next, or a job ad you like. The page lists the skills, competences and job titles it recognises; tick the ones you want and add them. The text itself is not saved in the repository.

Ranking

How strict the shortlist is.

Minimum match score
Jobs below this score are not shown.
Company rating bonus
Points added for the company's rating in the register.

Weights of the match score

How much each part counts. The total is rescaled to 0–100, so only the proportions matter. Parts that don't apply (no CV text, no personal skills) are left out.

GitHub access

Saving writes config/profile.yaml in your repository. It needs an access token with Contents: Read and write on that repository – the same token as for “Sync across devices” on the job page. It stays in this browser only.
